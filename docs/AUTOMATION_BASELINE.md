**Evidence scope.** Code and test inspection establishes implementation paths. The audit did not inspect customer records, production logs, credentials, supplier contracts, private cloud billing or recovery snapshots. Production error rates, physical stock, live provider readiness and disaster recovery remain unverified. “Gap” means no end-to-end implementation was identified in these revisions, including when related components exist.

2. Verify the paired release in an approved isolated staging environment. Use a real provider *test* account for prepaid and courier booking and callbacks. Operators supply pickup/pincode eligibility, parcel weights, courier charges, supplier terms and approved destinations privately. Do not activate production on local results.
3. Address parcel-level collections and RTO, reverse logistics, refund and remittance exceptions, and independently sourced ledger reconciliation before permitting unattended financial effects. Review actual invoicing entity and tax treatment with the operator and accountant. A markup figure alone cannot establish net profit.
4. Extend CRM, marketing, mobile synchronization, loyalty, referrals, supplier risk, forecasting, and privacy operations according to their row-level prerequisites. Validate consent semantics, approved templates, incentive policies, sufficient observed data and actual device operation.

No new paid subscriptions, real shipments, ad spend or transfers were authorized by these changes. Existing host compute, database/index storage, CI and any eventual per-message, shipping or inference fees are usage-dependent.

## Launch dependencies

1. Both the older supplier portal and newer marketplace exist. Backend `docs/MARKETPLACE_SETUP.md` directs operators to use one commercial workflow per supplier. The newer path has a feature flag, requires a transactional MongoDB replica set and creates entitlements, fulfilments and ledger entries together. Preserve these transactions when adding downstream events.
2. Existing prepaid and COD-advance workflows carry purchased amounts, stock reservations, gateway references and refund history. New ₹0-upfront COD needs its own payment semantics, backend capability indication and a fixture proving old receipts keep their balances. A frontend label alone cannot change checkout.
3. Actual supplier identity, approved customer and supplier amounts, variant-level stock, pickup location, carrier terms, return responsibility and payout destination require evidence from the operator. The launch register in storefront `docs/OPERATIONS_ACCEPTANCE.md` describes the required inputs. Database catalogue quantities cannot establish physical availability.
4. Ordinary reconciliation can run automatically once the runner, data and source of truth have been verified. Retain the existing distinction between supplier earnings, transfer reservations, externally completed transfers and subsequent reversals. No credentials or rights to move funds are implied by the existence of a controller.
5. Marketing has its own retrying, leased Meta-event worker. Preserve its consent, opt-outs and external deduplication contract. A general job engine should initially handle independent, bounded, recoverable internal work. Retry must never blindly repeat a capture, shipment, refund or payout.
6. Read-only GitHub review found overlapping backend PRs #6 (account/shopping/notification/staging), #16 (public fulfilment and customer updates), #17 (brand artwork). Frontend #57 pairs with #16, #63 with #17. Review #46/#51/#54/#58 for iPhone, #56 for catalogue/support/accessibility, #68/#72 for storefront work. Open PRs are not evidence of deployment.
7. Existing pricing and return windows are stored on purchased records. Marketplace setup documents a 1–7-day purchase-window range and a seven-day default. A proposed 72-hour policy must be checked against the actual purchase, published policy and applicable rights before use.

## Phased delivery and update protocol

First deliver the isolated internal workflow runner and compatible zero-advance COD. Confirm new and historical orders before exercising existing marketplace fulfilment and ledger logic with two suppliers. Establish live-approved logistics and reconciliation inputs before activating bookings, remittance handling or outbound messages. Build support, growth and owner reporting on these verified events. Keep optional loyalty, referrals and forecasting behind agreed business rules.

For each module record branch/PR, tests and exact environment, deployment flag, owner, external dependency, acceptance result and next action. Future flags move through **proposed → implemented → fixture-tested → staging verified → approved → deployed → production verified**. Record failure or blocker separately at any stage. Production cutover, provider activation, paid services, refunds, payouts, destructive migrations and policy changes require owner approval.

## Provider and legal dependencies

**Shipping.** Shiprocket documents multi-courier pincode serviceability and AWB assignment. Delhivery One documents B2C serviceability, estimated cost, manifestation, waybill, label, pickup and tracking; its client portal explicitly supports staging API tests. Obtain each provider’s account-specific pickup eligibility, forward/reverse rate card, volumetric divisor, COD fees and settlement terms. Compare the same supplier pickup, delivery PIN, parcel dimensions, weights and COD amount. Carrier reliability requires observed data. No provider was selected or connected using a public headline rate. [Shiprocket API](https://apidocs.shiprocket.in/) · [Delhivery B2C](https://one.delhivery.com/developer-portal/documents/b2c/) · [Delhivery staging access](https://help.delhivery.com/docs/client-developer-portal-1)

**Indian privacy and consumer workflows.** The November 2025 DPDP commencement notifications stagger obligations. As of November 2026 the one-year tranche has arrived, including the Consent Manager provision and Rule 4. The broad processing, notices and rights provisions and much of the operational rules are scheduled at eighteen months after Gazette publication, in May 2027. Read subsequent amendments and the actual entity’s roles with qualified advisers before release. The E-Commerce Rules require a grievance mechanism; use a timer for acknowledgement within 48 hours and redress within one month, with human escalation. Preserve an exception path for statutory consumer remedies independently of an ordinary exchange window. Seller identity, invoice issuer, GST registrations, applicable rates and document retention require factual and professional confirmation. No legal certification or changes to customer policy are recorded by this audit. [DPDP Act commencement notification](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf) · [DPDP Rules](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf) · [Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/E%20commerce%20rules.pdf)

The 2026 Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) amendment is scheduled to apply from 1 January 2027. Recheck its commencement and later amendments at release.
